DESIGN NOTES

Albums come first.

Closed, illustrated covers keep photographs and videos out of the arrival screen. Titles, counts and sections help you choose a destination before seeing its contents.

Search stays within the current space or album. Unorganised files have a deliberate route. Adding files always names the destination.

This local HTML concept uses sample content. Changes reset on reload. Workspace switching simulates separate libraries; it does not implement access control. Albums organise files; they do not create privacy permissions.
